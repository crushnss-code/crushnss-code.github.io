Make, review and schedule YouTube Shorts from your own PC.

ClipForge is a private desktop application for creating, reviewing, scheduling and publishing YouTube Shorts on your own YouTube channels.

ClipForge runs on your Windows computer. It is not a hosted service. There is no ClipForge account to sign up for, and there is no ClipForge server. Your videos, settings and YouTube sign-ins stay on your computer.

Create

Turn your stories, narration and footage into vertical Shorts with captions, on your own PC.

Review

Watch every Short and see automatic quality checks before anything is uploaded.

Schedule

Pick posting times for each channel. ClipForge finds free slots that don't clash with your other uploads.

Publish

Upload approved Shorts to YouTube as private videos that go public at the time you chose.

Why ClipForge asks to access your YouTube account

To upload and schedule Shorts, ClipForge connects to your channel through the YouTube Data API, using Google's sign-in (OAuth 2.0). You choose the channel on Google's sign-in page. ClipForge uses that access only to do what you ask it to do:

  • show which channel is connected (its name, handle and profile picture);
  • upload the videos you approve, with their title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy setting and publishing time;
  • change the schedule or details of videos that ClipForge uploaded;
  • read the status and schedule of your channel's recent uploads, to confirm that YouTube accepted each video and to avoid scheduling clashes.

ClipForge never deletes your videos and never reads or changes your comments, ratings or captions. It does not use your data for advertising, and it does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

Read the Privacy Policy to see exactly what ClipForge stores, how the sign-in is protected, and how to remove it. By using ClipForge, you agree to the Terms of Service and the YouTube Terms of Service.
